# Popularity board frames (owner-supplied, 2026-10-25)

Supplied by the owner as Kabso's frames for the top three of the popularity board (CP-016 A,
D99): gold (1), blue (2), rose (3). Each was a 1254x1254 transparent image with the rank number
drawn in; resized to 420x420 (Lanczos, 3x of the largest 104 pt they are drawn at, plus margin)
and saved as WebP (quality 88). Not otherwise modified. The originals stay outside git.
