# Tap characters (owner-supplied, 2026-10-25)

Supplied by the owner in the tap likes pack (`kabso_tap_likes.zip`): the ten Kabi characters of
the charm tiers (D101) as 112x112 PNGs, used as they are. They pop where a viewer taps the live
(`LiveTapLayer`); a room shows only the tiers its host's charm level has reached. The pack's
preview video stays outside git.

`tap_01_sprout.png` … `tap_10_royal.png`: sprout, bunny, kitty, butterfly, fox, owl, unicorn,
dragon, phoenix, royal (tiers 1–10).
