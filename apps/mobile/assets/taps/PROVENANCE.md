# Tap characters (owner-supplied, 2026-10-25)

Supplied by the owner in the tap likes pack (`kabso_tap_likes.zip`): the ten Kabi characters of
the charm tiers (D101) as 112x112 PNGs, used as they are. They pop where a viewer or the host
taps the live (`LiveTapLayer`); since D120 (owner, 2026-10-26) every tap picks one of all ten at
random, whatever the host's charm level, drawn at 19 pt in a narrow lane at the right edge. The pack's preview video stays outside git.

`tap_01_sprout.png` … `tap_10_royal.png`: sprout, bunny, kitty, butterfly, fox, owl, unicorn,
dragon, phoenix, royal (tiers 1–10).
