(D256): gold «TOP 1» (red gems), silver «TOP 2» (blue gems), bronze «TOP 3» (purple gems). Each was a

(Lanczos, about 3× the largest size they are drawn at) and saved as WebP (quality 90). Not otherwise
modified. The originals stay outside git.
