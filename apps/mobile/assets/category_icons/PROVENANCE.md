# Live category icons (owner-supplied, 2026-10-24)

Supplied by the owner as Kabso's icons for the live categories (one 1536x1024 sheet with five
tiles: Chat, Dating, Games, Interests, Emotional). Each icon was cut from its tile, its tile
background removed (a plane fitted to the crop's edge, distance-based alpha), and saved as a
96x96 transparent PNG (3x of the 32 pt it is drawn at, at most). Not otherwise modified.
The owner's sheet itself stays outside git.
