# Charm level icons (owner-supplied, 2026-10-25)

Supplied by the owner in the charm levels pack (`kabso_charm_levels.zip`): ten 3D Kabi creatures,
one per tier of the charm (`ATTRACTIVENESS`) level, levels 1–10, 11–20 … 91–100 (D101). These
are the pack's tight-cropped 256x256 `icon/` PNGs, resized to 96x96 (3x of the 32 pt they are
drawn at, at most). Not otherwise modified. The pack's 512 px PNGs, Lottie animations and preview
videos stay outside git.
