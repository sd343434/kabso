# Live gift effects (CP-020, D108): waiting for the owner

Full-screen gift effects, named by a gift's `animationKey`: `assets/gifts/anim/<animationKey>.svga`
(SVGA 2.0, like `assets/battle/`). The room plays it centred, muted, once, within 3.2 s. Without
the file the gift's image grows in and fades out instead.
