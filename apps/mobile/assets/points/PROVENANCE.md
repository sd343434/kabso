# Points coin (owner-supplied, 2026-10-25)

Supplied by the owner as Kabso's icon for live points (CP-012), in place of the flame: a gold
coin with a crowned "K". The original was a 1503x1046 image on black; the black background
outside the coin was removed (dark pixels reached from the image edges, a 3 px soft edge with
the black divided out), the coin cropped to its bounds, centred on a square and resized to
96x96 PNG (3x of the 32 pt it is drawn at, at most). Not otherwise modified. The original stays
outside git.
