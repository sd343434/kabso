Country flags (D84 leaderboard; D140: every ISO 3166-1 code, for the end-of-live summary's viewer
countries): the flag glyphs of Noto Color Emoji (Google, https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji),
rendered to 72 × 54 px WebP (lower-case alpha-2 code, e.g. `sa.webp`). Noto Color Emoji is licensed
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the flag images in noto-emoji are in the public domain. The
OFL text is the same as in assets/fonts/OFL-Inter.txt.
